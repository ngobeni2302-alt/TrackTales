Flagship 1: Ultra Luxury Express

The Blue Train

"A Window to the Soul of South Africa"

Synonymous with world-class luxury rail travel since 1946. The Blue Train offers 24-hour butler service, marble-tiled ensuite bathrooms with full bathtubs, silver-service multi-course dining, and panoramic observation windows looking out across 1,600 kilometers of spectacular African landscape.

SPEED 90 km/h (Smooth Slow Travel)
DURATION 31 Hours (Pretoria ➔ Cape Town)
RATING 5-Star Luxury Butler Service
DINING Gourmet 5-Course & SA Wines

Key Luxury Highlights:

  • 24/7 Dedicated Personal Butler Service for every carriage suite
  • En-suite marble bathrooms with full-sized porcelain bathtub
  • Cigar & Cognac Club Lounge Car with plush leather armchairs
  • Guided stopover excursion to the historic Kimberley Big Hole Diamond Mine

Flagship 2: Vintage Edwardian Safari

Rovos Rail

"The Most Luxurious Train in the World"

Established in 1989 by Rohan Vos, Rovos Rail recaptures the nostalgia and Edwardian elegance of 1920s train travel. Featuring hand-restored wood-paneled suites, open-air balcony observation cars for photography, formal evening dress codes, and high tea served daily.

SPEED 60 km/h (Nostalgic Slow Safari)
DURATION 48 Hours (Relaxed Safari Pace)
VIEWING Open-Air Rear Balcony Car
DRESS Formal Gala Evening Banquets

Key Luxury Highlights:

  • Rear Observation Car with open glass doors and outdoor balcony terrace
  • Restored 1920s Edwardian teak wood craftsmanship and brass fittings
  • Royal Suites occupying half a carriage (16 sq metres) with Victorian bath
  • Off-train historic walk through Matjiesfontein preserved Victorian village

About TrackTales & South Africa Tourism

Connecting Heritage, Tourism & Rail

South Africa’s rail network is not just transport—it is the historical backbone that shaped our nation's cities, mining landmarks, and diverse cultural landscapes.

What is TrackTales?

TrackTales is an immersive digital platform celebrating the stories, sights, and luxury of South Africa's railways. We bridge heritage rail history with modern digital discovery, giving travelers, tourists, and enthusiasts a front-row seat to Mzansi's rail corridor.

South Africa Tourism

From the Jacaranda-lined streets of Pretoria and the gold mines of Egoli to the starry Karoo desert night skies and Table Mountain’s coastal ocean views. Rail travel offers an eco-friendly, leisurely, and breathtaking perspective of South Africa’s natural beauty.

Rail Transportation Legacy

Laid during the 1870s diamond rush in Kimberley and gold fever in Johannesburg, South Africa's rail lines connected inland mining hub cities directly to coastal ports, laying the foundation for modern trade, architecture, and iconic luxury trains like The Blue Train and Rovos Rail.

Why Travel South Africa By Rail?

A timeless journey across 1,600 kilometers of Mzansi heritage

Railway transportation in South Africa represents over 150 years of engineering marvels and human passion. As your train travels across the vast Highveld plains, through the mountain tunnels of the Hex River Valley, and down to the Atlantic coastline of Cape Town, you experience the changing topography and rich cultural tapestry of nine provinces.

Cultural Preserved Stops

Explore historical Victorian stations like Matjiesfontein, preserved in time since 1884.

Culinary & Wine Pairings

Savor regional delicacies from Karoo Lamb and biltong to award-winning Western Cape Pinotage wines.

Eco-Friendly Travel

Passenger rail offers a sustainable, low-carbon alternative to explore South Africa’s wilderness.
