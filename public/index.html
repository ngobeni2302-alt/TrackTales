What is TrackTales?

TrackTales is a digital companion for the Pretoria to Cape Town corridor, making luxury rail heritage accessible to everyone through stories, audio soundscapes, and sight solvers.

South Africa Tourism

From Jacaranda streets in Pretoria and Kimberley diamond vaults to the starry Karoo night sky and Table Mountain's coastal peak, rail travel showcases Mzansi at its finest.

Rail Legacy

Laid during the 1870s diamond rush, the Pretoria to Cape Town line connected inland hubs directly to coastal ports, creating legendary trains like The Blue Train and Rovos Rail.
