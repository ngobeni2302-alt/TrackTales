Blyde River Escarpment, Mpumalanga

God's Window & Panorama Route

Perched high on the Drakensberg escarpment, God's Window offers a breathtaking 900-meter sheer drop looking over the lush indigenous rainforest and Blyde River Canyon. On clear days, the view extends into Mozambique.

Key Attraction Highlights

  • • 900m sheer vertical drop overlooking pristine rainforest
  • • Gateway to Lisbon & Berlin Waterfalls
  • • Panoramas extending across the Lowveld to Mozambique
